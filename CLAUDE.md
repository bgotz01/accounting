# AI Financial Intelligence Platform — Build Specification

## Project Overview

We are building an AI-native financial intelligence platform for business owners.

This is NOT traditional accounting software.

The product is designed for operators and founders who want:

* clarity,
* visibility,
* operational intelligence,
* and AI-generated business guidance.

The core workflow is:

1. User uploads raw business data
2. AI structures and categorizes the data
3. System generates financial insights and operational intelligence
4. User interacts conversationally with the business data

The UX should feel closer to:

* ChatGPT
* Notion AI
* modern AI copilots

…than traditional ERP/accounting software.

---

# Core Product Principles

## 1. Built for Owners, Not Accountants

Avoid:

* accounting-heavy UX
* complex bookkeeping terminology
* ERP-style workflows
* enterprise clutter

Prioritize:

* clarity
* summaries
* actionable guidance
* conversational interaction
* operational visibility

---

## 2. Raw Data First

The system must support messy inputs.

Users should be able to upload:

* CSVs
* Excel files
* PDFs
* invoices
* receipts
* bank exports
* financial statements

Minimal onboarding.

The AI handles normalization and organization.

---

## 3. AI-Native Architecture

AI is not an add-on.

The system is fundamentally designed around:

* extraction,
* classification,
* synthesis,
* interpretation,
* and reasoning.

The AI layer is the core product.

# 4. Dashboard

The dashboard is NOT an accounting dashboard.

It is a business intelligence dashboard.

## Initial Sections

### Financial Health

* total cash in
* total cash out
* net cash flow
* monthly trend
* largest expenses
* recurring expenses

### Risk Signals

* customer concentration
* expense spikes
* declining margins
* unusual transactions
* negative cash flow streaks

### AI Summary

Natural language summary generated by AI.

Example:

"Revenue remained stable this month, but supplier costs increased 18%. Marketing spend is rising faster than customer growth. Cash runway is approximately 5.2 months at current burn."
